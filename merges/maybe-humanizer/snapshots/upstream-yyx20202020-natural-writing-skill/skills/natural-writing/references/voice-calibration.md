# Voice Calibration

Build a compact voice profile from user-provided samples.

## Observe

- Sentence-length distribution and paragraph density
- Vocabulary register and tolerance for technical terms
- Directness, hedging, humor, disagreement, and emotional range
- Preferred openings, transitions, examples, and endings
- Punctuation, parentheses, headings, lists, and fragments
- How the author distinguishes facts, guesses, and opinions

## Produce a profile

Record:

1. Five stable traits supported by examples.
2. Three habits to avoid overfitting.
3. A short do/don't table.
4. Two brief calibration rewrites.

Use behaviors, not identity labels. Do not infer age, gender, ethnicity, health, politics, or private biography.

## Apply

- Match distributions rather than cloning memorable phrases.
- Preserve the target text's subject-matter register.
- Let factual and channel constraints override stylistic mimicry.
- If samples conflict, state that the voice is mixed and choose the traits relevant to the current channel.
