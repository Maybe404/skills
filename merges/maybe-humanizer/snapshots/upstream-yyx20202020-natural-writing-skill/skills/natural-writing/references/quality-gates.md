# Quality Gates

An edit passes only when every hard gate passes.

## Hard gates

- All exact protected spans are present or explicitly approved for change.
- Actors, responsibility, negation, conditions, confidence, and evidence relationships are unchanged.
- No anecdote, quotation, source, statistic, or personal experience was invented.
- The result fits the requested channel, audience, and register.
- The chosen intensity and scope were not exceeded.

## Quality checks

- Concrete claims outnumber unsupported significance claims.
- Paragraphs have distinct jobs and do not end with repeated summaries.
- Sentence rhythm follows the thought rather than a visible template.
- Transitions are necessary and varied.
- Terminology remains consistent where consistency matters.
- The result sounds like a person addressing this reader in this situation, not a model demonstrating writing.

## Delivery check

Read the result once without comparing it to the original, then once beside the original. The first pass catches awkward prose; the second catches lost meaning.

Do not use AI-detector scores as a hard gate. They are unstable proxies and cannot replace factual review or human preference testing.
